# Case Study 1: Windows Workstation Login Failure

**Ticket:** #178405  
**Organization:** Omerhi Technologies (fictional)  
**Department:** IT Help Desk  
**Priority:** High  
**Status:** Closed

## Incident Overview

A simulated employee, Sarah Johnson from the Finance Department, reported being unable to sign in to her Windows workstation. The issue prevented access to her work environment and required IT Help Desk assistance.

## Troubleshooting Approach

1. Reviewed the reported login symptoms and business impact.
2. Investigated potential credential and account-access problems within the simulated scenario.
3. Documented the troubleshooting process using internal ticket notes.
4. Simulated a password reset as the corrective action.
5. Recorded the simulated resolution and closed the ticket.

## Resolution

The fictional incident was resolved through a simulated password reset, restoring the employee's ability to access the workstation.
